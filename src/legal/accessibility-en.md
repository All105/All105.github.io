{{ site.business_name }} wants everyone to be able to use this website. Our goal is to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA**.

## What we have done

- Every page can be used with a keyboard. The services slideshow can be moved with the arrow keys and the on-screen buttons, and it can be paused.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, the slideshow does not animate or advance on its own.
- Text and buttons are designed to meet AA color-contrast levels; form fields have visible labels and error messages that are announced to screen readers.
- Images have text alternatives, and the whole site is available in English and Spanish.

## Known limitations

- The security check on the form (Cloudflare Turnstile) is provided by a third party; if it gives you trouble, call or email us instead and we will take your request.
- Some photos may still be placeholders while we replace them with our own.

## Report a problem

If you find a barrier on this website, or need information in another format, contact us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) or [{{ site.phone_display | nbphone }}](tel:{{ site.phone }}). Tell us the page and the problem, and we will help you by another channel while we fix it.
