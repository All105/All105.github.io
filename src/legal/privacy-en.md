## 2. Information we collect

**a) When you send the quote form:** your name, phone number, email address, type of service, ZIP code and the message you write; your confirmation that you read this policy; whether you checked the optional text-message (SMS) box, together with the date, time and exact wording you agreed to; the language of the page and the page you sent it from. Please do not include payment card, Social Security, driver's license or other sensitive numbers in the message.

**b) Security check:** the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated spam. Turnstile processes technical information from your browser (such as IP address and device and browser characteristics) to decide whether the request comes from a person. We only receive a pass/fail result. Our form service (a Cloudflare Worker) sees your IP address while processing the request, uses it only for that check and to limit abuse, and does not store it.

**c) Technical data needed to deliver the site:** like any website, our hosting and security providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) process technical request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, in order to deliver pages and protect the site. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies when it detects suspicious traffic. Our own website does not set cookies.

<span id="analytics"></span>**d) Visit statistics — only if you allow them:** if you choose "Allow" in the analytics notice, we load Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and reports aggregate information (pages visited, referring site, country, browser type and page speed). It does not use cookies and does not identify you. If you choose "Do not allow", or if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, it is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the "Analytics preferences" link in the footer.

**e) Preferences stored on your device:** we save your language choice and your analytics choice in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

**f) If you call, text, email or message us on WhatsApp:** we receive the information you choose to send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta under its own terms and privacy policy.

## 3. How we use it

- To answer your request, prepare a quote and, if you hire us, schedule and provide the service.
- To send you text messages about your quote and appointments, **only** if you checked the SMS box.
- To protect the website and the form against spam and abuse.
- To comply with legal obligations and keep records required by law.

We do **not** sell your personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you:

- **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS) — delivers the email that notifies us of your request and, if enabled, the confirmation email sent to you.
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** — content delivery, security, the Turnstile security check, the Worker that processes the form, and visit statistics if you allow them.
- **GitHub, Inc.** — hosts the website files.
- **Google LLC (Gmail)** — our business mailbox, where quote requests are received.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business. Some providers may process information outside the United States.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and related messages for up to **{{ site.legal.retention_months }} months** after our last contact with you, and then delete them, unless we need them longer because you became a customer, for tax or accounting records, or to resolve a dispute. Records showing that you agreed to receive text messages are kept for as long as you are subscribed and for up to 4 years after you opt out, so that we can prove consent if required. Brevo and Cloudflare keep technical logs for limited periods under their own policies.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You can ask us to **access, correct or delete** the personal information we hold about you by writing to [{{ site.legal.privacy_email }}](mailto:{{ site.legal.privacy_email }}) or calling [{{ site.phone_display | nbphone }}](tel:{{ site.phone }}). We may need to confirm your identity before we act on the request, and we aim to respond within 45 days. We may keep information we are legally required to keep.

- **Text messages:** reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving texts, or **HELP** for help.
- **Emails:** use the unsubscribe link included in our emails.
- **Analytics:** use the "Analytics preferences" link in the footer.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS on every page, strict security headers, bot protection on the form and two-factor authentication on the accounts that manage the website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data breach notice

If a breach of security affects personal information as defined by Florida law (section 501.171, Florida Statutes), we will notify affected individuals as required by that law, no later than 30 days after we determine that a breach occurred, unless a law enforcement agency asks us to delay, and we will notify the Florida Department of Legal Affairs when the law requires it. To report a suspected security problem with this website, write to [{{ site.legal.privacy_email }}](mailto:{{ site.legal.privacy_email }}).

## 9. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles personal information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
